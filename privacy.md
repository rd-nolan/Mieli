# **Privacy Policy**

**Last Updated: August 25, 2026**

Mieli respects your privacy. Mieli is designed as a local-first note-taking application for macOS, and we do not collect, store, transmit, or share your personal data.

## **Data Collection**

Mieli does not collect any personal information or usage data.

We do not collect:

- Personal information
- Your notes or written content
- Usage or analytics data
- Device information
- Location information
- Advertising identifiers
- Diagnostic or tracking data

## **Local Data Storage**

All notes and content you create in Mieli are stored locally on your Mac.

Mieli does not upload your notes or other content to our servers, and we do not have access to the content you create in the app.

You are responsible for managing and backing up the data stored on your device.

## **Third-Party Tracking**

Mieli does not use advertising SDKs, analytics services, or third-party tracking technologies.

## **Data Sharing**

Because Mieli does not collect your personal data, we do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties.

## **Changes to This Privacy Policy**

We may update this Privacy Policy if Mieli’s features or data practices change. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

## **Contact**

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the Mieli GitHub repository.
